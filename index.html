About me

I'm a researcher and builder working with language models. My background is in natural language processing. For my bachelor's thesis I worked on summarising long scientific papers with transformers, and in 2024 I joined a research program at Sharif University of Technology on LLM-based tutoring systems.

What I care about most is how people learn. I was a teaching assistant for five courses during my degree, and since 2024 I've co-run AI Agents Simplified, a newsletter that explains agents, LLMs and RAG in plain terms to more than 57,000 readers. I like understanding how a system works from the inside, and that's what I try to pass on when I write.
